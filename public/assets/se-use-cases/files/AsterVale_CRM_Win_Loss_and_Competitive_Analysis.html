Next moves

Turn the signal into a decision test.

Recommendations are specific to AsterVale’s supplied priorities and the loss patterns; each should produce evidence an evaluator can inspect.

Stage the cross-task proof

Use one authorized engagement source in a proposal task, then a separate client-briefing task. Show the source/version reference, a correction, the same approved context reused, and a missing-fact refusal. Let the evaluator inspect each native file. This directly tests the differentiators reflected in VelaBridge, Rainford, Cloudmarsh and SableWing.

Owner: solution lead · Evidence: saved test outputs

Make the gates explicit before asking for preference

Have IT and Security record a go/no-go view on manual provisioning, offboarding, group behavior, processing route, subprocessors, assurance evidence and the incident-notice clause. The HarborElm / Greenhaven losses show SCIM/access requirements can be decisive; JuniperSpan shows late assurance can close the window (its source was seller-relayed).

Owners: customer IT + security; Noumi response owners

Separate workflow memory from records governance

Ask whether AsterVale primarily needs engagement-specific context or a controlled reference library with named owners, approval, retention and legal holds. Demo client separation and source versioning, but do not claim a project workspace is a records-management system. Give Crestline’s governance case a fair, explicit test.

Owner: business sponsor · Evidence: agreed acceptance rubric

Measure the editable handoff, not first-draft speed

Score DOCX/XLSX/PPTX editability, evidence traceability, factual corrections and reviewer reconstruction time. Include one representative PowerPoint layout, state unsupported animation/object limits, and count failed tasks and rework. Keep capacity value distinct from cash savings.

Owners: workflow leads · Evidence: pre-agreed pilot log

De-risk budget and decision ownership

Normalize scope and total effort behind the stated $3,600 year-one / $6,000 recurring supplier-fee difference; usage units are not comparable. Confirm budget holder, legal approvers, pilot conditions and a dated selection path so a positive demo cannot turn into a sponsorless No Decision.

Owner: account director · Evidence: normalized quote + action register

Keep a credible alternative case

If Notion write-back, centralized lifecycle controls or SCIM are hard requirements, state the gap and quantify any workaround/SOW before bid approval. LindenArc’s 60-seat loss is the closest current warning; do not let the workflow win conceal an integration dependency.

Owner: opportunity team · Evidence: scoped gap disposition

Limitations that matter

Read these constraints before using any historical signal.

  • No AsterVale CRM rowIn the 40-record extract, no CRM stage, probability or recorded outcome for AV-2026-041 can be verified.
  • Source-date conflictThis report is dated 30 Sep 2026. The opportunity brief says snapshot 1 Oct; the comparison workbook cites Addendum 01 dated 9 Oct and a Noumi quote issued 14 Oct; a supplied meeting transcript is dated 22 Oct and describes a later conditional pilot. Those later materials are supplied evidence, not verified current state as of this report date. The brief and transcript stages do not reconcile.
  • Small, selected cohortsThe sample contains 9 strict direct analogues and 18 adjacent records. It is purposefully selected using known AsterVale dimensions; it is not random or statistically representative.
  • Qualitative reason codingCRM quotes and reasons are not quantified impact measures. A few are seller-relayed or internal-only; labels are retained. “Closed Lost” does not establish one universal technical cause.
  • Version and market driftThe CRM spans Noumi releases 2025.4–2026.3 and Crestline Workbench 6.0–6.2. The closest direct 6.2 comparison is LindenArc, which was lost; SableWing won against 6.1, explicitly not 6.2.
  • Commercial comparability is limitedCRM cost fields compare Noumi fees only, excluding competitor schedules, taxes, usage overages and customer internal effort. AsterVale fee figures are not normalized scope or TCO.
  • Outcome biasNo-decision outcomes can reflect timing, budget, sponsor or readiness, not relative product merit. They are shown separately and are never recoded as losses.
  • Competitive comparison boundaryClaims reflect the supplied Noumi capability and security documents and the Crestline handbook. “Not described” is not evidence of absent capability. Product materials do not prove AsterVale configuration, contract acceptance or test performance.
